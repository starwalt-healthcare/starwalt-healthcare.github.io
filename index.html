HELPFUL BEFORE YOU VISIT

Your questions,
answered.

Practical information for a more comfortable hospital visit.

How does online appointment booking work?

Choose a specialty, doctor and preferred slot. This demo creates an on-screen preview only. A hospital implementation can connect requests to its booking system.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Bring identification, relevant previous reports, your current medication list and insurance details if applicable. Confirm any preparation requirements with your hospital.

Can I use my health insurance?

Your hospital can publish verified insurer and TPA partnerships, cashless admission procedures and assistance contact details here.

Are teleconsultations and home care available?

These service categories are included in the showcase. Actual availability, clinical suitability, service areas and charges depend on the hospital.

Is this a real hospital website?

This is a STARWALT Healthcare demonstration designed for hospitals to explore a customised website. The profiles, bookings, reports and packages are illustrative.
